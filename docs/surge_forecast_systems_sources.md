# National storm-surge forecast and warning services: sources

Companion to `catalog/surge_forecast_systems.csv` (137 coastal countries and territories). Search date: 2026-09-05. Compiled by a web survey with WebSearch/WebFetch plus direct reading of agency pages, WMO documents and peer-reviewed system descriptions; regional passes were run in parallel and merged. This file gives one evidence bullet per country in the form ISO3 Country | status | evidence sentence | URL(s).

## Methods note

**Question.** Does a NATIONAL operational storm-surge (or storm-tide / coastal-inundation / coastal-flood) forecast and warning service exist today, run by the national meteorological, hydrological or oceanographic agency or a national partner (navy hydrographic office, national university centre or civil-protection agency under formal mandate)?

**Status vocabulary.**
- `national`: the country's own agency runs an operational surge / storm-tide / coastal-inundation forecast or warning service. Accepted evidence: a numerical surge model run nationally (agency page, WMO document, peer-reviewed system description, official cooperation page), or a formal warning product that forecasts surge or coastal inundation (e.g. "storm surge warning", "coastal flood warning", quantitative surge heights in the agency's own cyclone alerts). Generic marine forecasts and swell / high-wave / spring-tide warnings alone do not count; they are recorded in the notes.
- `regional_only`: the only documented surge information reaches the country from a WMO Regional Specialized Meteorological Centre (RSMC Miami/NHC international products, RSMC New Delhi, RSMC La Reunion, RSMC Nadi, RSMC Tokyo) or a foreign or donor-run centre, and the national agency relays it or has no surge product of its own. CIFDP-Caribbean (Dominican Republic, Haiti) is placed here because the surge scenarios are NHC SLOSH products.
- `none_found`: no source for a surge or inundation product was found within a few searches; the notes state what the agency does offer. `none_found` is a statement about this search pass, not proof of absence.

**Source preference.** Agency web pages, WMO documents (Coastal Inundation Forecasting Demonstration Project / Initiative reports and WMO Bulletin, Tropical Cyclone Committee and Panel on Tropical Cyclones reports, JMA/WMO workshop country reports), peer-reviewed system descriptions, and official cooperation pages; state or national news agencies were used when the agency page was unreachable and the news item quotes the agency's product. Only URLs that appeared in search results or were fetched are cited.

**Limits.** The session's web-search quota was exhausted part-way through the long tail, so several small states rest on one or two searches plus direct fetches (stated in their notes). Several agency sites blocked automated access (403, bot checks) or were down on the search date (PMD Pakistan, DMH Myanmar, BMKG InaCIFS, Marinha do Brasil, ReliefWeb); those rows cite the best reachable source and say so. Borderline calls are marked in the bullets: PAK (warning product verified, model provenance not), MMR (a "Storm Surge Warning" menu entry exists but the page is empty), OMN (agency describes hydrodynamic surge simulations, no named model), COL (announced for the 2025 season, product page not located), CUB (INSMET journal and TV-reported surge forecasts; .cu sites unreachable), SGP (research-operational system, no public MSS warning product), WSM (Coastal Flood Advisory product, no model), NZL (NIWA service, nationwide warning product unconfirmed), IRL, LVA, LTU, GRC, ROU and ITA (sea-level or water-level forecast/warning products without a dedicated national storm-surge warning, or Adriatic-only coverage), PRT (IPMA sea-surface-height maps of unstated provenance), PAN and GUY (tide-driven coastal-flood advisories, no surge model), CIV and GHA (press-reported wave-threshold coastal warnings). Two rows were reclassified in a consistency audit after the regional passes (PNG and KIR, both to none_found; the bullets carry the original call and the reason). The audit overlay is kept in `catalog/_parts/audit_overrides.csv`.

**Tally.** national 48, regional_only 35, none_found 54 (137 rows).
